BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A little
more detail.

What can I dissect right now?+

The onion lab is ready to explore, with a supported incision, removable layers, and a thin epidermis patch. Frog anatomy and microscopic exploration are planned for later.

How do I control the specimen?+

Drag to inspect, scroll to zoom, and choose the scalpel or forceps to cut and peel. Right drag rotates the camera while using a tool. Keyboard controls are available too.

Can I cut anywhere?+

The current onion supports one guided longitudinal incision. Short strokes add up. Once the seam is open, use the forceps to lift the section and peel the exposed layers.

Do I need to install anything?+

No app installation is needed. Open the lab in a browser with WebGL support. A laptop with a mouse or trackpad works best.
